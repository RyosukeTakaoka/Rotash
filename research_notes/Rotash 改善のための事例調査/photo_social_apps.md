# Friend-based photo/social apps: retention, engagement loops, and why they declined (for Rotash)

Research date: 2026-10-16. Method note: WebFetch could not resolve any host in this session (DNS errors on techcrunch.com, wikipedia.org, prtimes.jp, comnico.jp), so every finding below comes from search-engine result summaries of the cited pages, not a full read of each page. Numbers have been cross-checked between sources where possible, and conflicts are flagged. Most hard user data is 2021–2025; very little public data exists for 2026.

## 1. BeReal: mechanic, peak and decline, why users left, later changes

### Takeaway
BeReal's single daily random prompt produced explosive growth in 2022 (App of the Year; MAU about 73.5M at its August 2022 peak) but daily use collapsed about 60% within roughly six months. One mandatory moment a day did not give people enough reason to come back once the novelty wore off. After Voodoo bought it in June 2024, BeReal has been adding more reasons to open the app (extra "Bonus" posts, video, chat, a celebrity feed, ads) and loosening its original single-moment purity.

### Cited Findings
- **Mechanic.** Once a day, at a random time, every user gets a notification that a two-minute posting window is open. The timing changes daily and is not known in advance. — [Wikipedia: BeReal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BeReal)
- **Gate.** Users cannot see any of the day's BeReals until they upload their own (a reciprocity lock). — [Wikipedia: BeReal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BeReal)
- **Late posting.** Posting after the window is allowed, but friends see the post marked "late". Critics said this undercut the app's authenticity premise. — [Wikipedia: BeReal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BeReal)
- **Award.** BeReal won Apple's iPhone App of the Year for 2022 (announced 2022-11-29). — [TechCrunch, 2022-11-29](https://techcrunch.com/2022/11/29/apple-announces-winners-of-the-app-store-awards-for-2022)
- **Peak.** MAU was about 73.5M in August 2022. By January 2024 it was about 23M active users. — [Fast Company, 2024-06](https://www.fastcompany.com/91142485/voodoo-bereal-monetization)
- **DAU fell about 61% from October 2022 to March 2023**, per Sensor Tower data reported by the NYT. — [Tubefilter, 2024-06-12](https://www.tubefilter.com/2024/06/12/game-company-voodoo-acquires-bereal-500-million/)
- **The DAU figures disagree.** Wikipedia gives about 15M DAU in October 2022, falling to about 6M by late spring 2023. — [Wikipedia: BeReal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BeReal). An aggregator gives 10.4M DAU in February 2023 and 6M in March 2023, with no primary source. — [Appic Softwares](https://appicsoftwares.com/blog/bereal-statistics/). Some aggregators say about 20M DAU in October 2022. — [Helplama](https://helplama.com/bereal-usage-statistics/). Treat all of these as estimates.
- **Sensor Tower (April 2023).** After peaking in 2022, monthly downloads "steadily declined", though MAU was still about 50M. — [Sensor Tower blog, "Getting real about BeReal"](https://sensortower.com/blog/getting-real-about-bereal)
- **Sensor Tower (July 2024, around the Voodoo deal).** Daily time spent was down 6% year on year, and sessions were down 14%. — [Sensor Tower on X](https://x.com/SensorTower/status/1808542259765465157)
- **Acquisition.** Voodoo acquired BeReal in June 2024 for a reported €500M (about $537M). — [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2024/06/12/game-company-voodoo-acquires-bereal-500-million/); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BeReal). TechCrunch reported BeReal had about 10 months of runway left and needed to raise money or be acquired. — [TechCrunch "Deal Dive", 2024-06-15](https://techcrunch.com/2024/06/15/deal-dive-bereal-got-its-best-case-scenario-exit). About 28 staff were laid off weeks after the deal. — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/bereal-hit-by-layoffs-following-voodoo-acquisition/)
- **Usage after the deal.**
  - BeReal said it had 40M MAU (about 5M in the US) when it rolled out US ads in April 2025. TechCrunch could not verify this figure. — [TechCrunch, 2025-04-10](https://techcrunch.com/2025/04/10/bereal-which-says-it-has-40m-monthly-users-is-rolling-out-ads-in-the-u-s/embed)
  - Business of Apps puts 2024 active users at about 16M. — [Business of Apps](https://www.businessofapps.com/data/bereal-statistics/)
  - Third-party DAU estimates are around 3M. — [Charle Agency summary](https://www.charleagency.com/articles/bereal-statistics/) (aggregator; low confidence)
- **Monetization and features under Voodoo.**
  - Ads started in-app in July 2024, and a US ad platform launched in April 2025. Voodoo reported about €30M of BeReal revenue for 2025. — [Marketing Brew, 2025-07-16](https://www.marketingbrew.com/stories/2025/07/16/bereal-comeback-strategy); [Charle Agency](https://www.charleagency.com/articles/bereal-statistics/)
  - New features include video and chat, plus bug and UI fixes. — [Marketing Brew](https://www.marketingbrew.com/stories/2025/07/16/bereal-comeback-strategy)
  - "Bonus BeReal": if you post on time, you get up to two extra posts that day, whenever you choose. — [Flinque blog](https://www.flinque.com/blog/bereal-update-everything-you-need-to-know/) (single blog source; lightly verified)
  - "RealPeople": a feed of unfiltered posts from notable actors, athletes and musicians. — [Flinque blog](https://www.flinque.com/blog/bereal-update-everything-you-need-to-know/) (lightly verified)
- **Reasons given for the decline.** Novelty wearing off, plus frequent crashes and glitches. — [Helplama](https://helplama.com/bereal-usage-statistics/); [em360tech](https://em360tech.com/tech-articles/what-happened-bereal-authenticity-obscurity); [Pedestrian.tv](https://www.pedestrian.tv/culture/bereal-decline-online-authenticity). These are commentary, not survey data.

### Inferences
- BeReal is the closest precedent for Rotash testers' complaint that there's "only one reason to open it per day". BeReal's own answer after the decline was to add more optional posts (Bonus BeReal) and more content to consume (RealPeople, video, chat). That is evidence that one rigid daily moment was not enough on its own.
- BeReal's reciprocity lock (post first, then see friends) is a strong daily driver, but it only works when every member has a daily action. In Rotash only one person shoots each day, so the other six have no action and nothing gated. That structural gap matches the tester complaint.
- Sessions fell faster than time spent (−14% vs −6% YoY). That suggests the daily trigger lost power before the content stopped being interesting.

### Gaps
- No primary Sensor Tower time series for BeReal DAU from 2022 to 2025 was available. The 2025–2026 DAU figures are aggregator estimates.
- No user survey was found that gives specific reasons for leaving. "Boring friends" and "nothing happens in my day" are commonly asserted, but no source with data was found.
- The details of RealMojis (a reaction selfie, launched 2022) are well known, but this session found no dated source for them.

## 2. Locket Widget: mechanic, growth, retention, Celebrity Lockets

### Takeaway
Locket turns the phone's home screen into the feed. Friends' photos appear in a widget without anyone opening the app, and replying takes "two clicks". That produced a viral launch (2M users in two weeks in January 2022) and, per the company, about 9–10M DAU in 2025 with "best-in-class D30 retention". The design caps the circle at about 20 close friends.

### Cited Findings
- **Origin.** Matt Moss built Locket as a gift for his girlfriend. A viral TikTok tutorial took it to No. 1 on the US App Store, with 2M users in two weeks. By end of 2022 it had more than 30M accounts. — [VnExpress International](https://e.vnexpress.net/news/tech/personalities/how-a-gift-for-his-girlfriend-changes-locket-founder-matt-moss-s-life-5106478.html); [Saba Substack](https://saba.substack.com/p/from-a-gift-for-his-girlfriend-to-d24)
- **Mechanic.** The widget shows near-real-time photos from up to 20 close friends and family. — [Fast Company, 2022](https://www.fastcompany.com/90818702/how-locket-a-widget-built-by-a-guy-as-a-gift-to-his-girlfriend-became-an-apple-app-store-award-winner); [Tech Funding News](https://techfundingnews.com/photo-sharing-app-founded-as-a-gift-to-girlfriend-sweeps-12-5m-funding/)
- **Low-friction reply.** Photos are taken in-app rather than picked from the camera roll. Moss: "When someone sends you something it's very natural to just tap the widget and in two clicks send the photo right back." — [Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/90818702/how-locket-a-widget-built-by-a-guy-as-a-gift-to-his-girlfriend-became-an-apple-app-store-award-winner)
- **Intimacy as positioning.** Moss says the app is dedicated to "those five or 10 people that they really care about" and is meant to make people feel closer to "the 10-15 people in their lives that matter the most". — [Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/90818702/how-locket-a-widget-built-by-a-guy-as-a-gift-to-his-girlfriend-became-an-apple-app-store-award-winner); [TFN](https://techfundingnews.com/photo-sharing-app-founded-as-a-gift-to-girlfriend-sweeps-12-5m-funding/)
- **Scale in 2025 (company claims).**
  - Over 80M downloads and over 9M DAU. — [TechCrunch, 2025-08-06](https://techcrunch.com/2025/08/06/photo-sharing-app-locket-is-banking-on-a-new-celebrity-focused-feature-to-fuel-its-growth)
  - Over 90M downloads and 9M DAU by October 2025. — [VnExpress](https://e.vnexpress.net/news/tech/personalities/how-a-gift-for-his-girlfriend-changes-locket-founder-matt-moss-s-life-5106478.html)
  - Moss's LinkedIn cites 10M DAU, over $10M ARR and "best-in-class D30 retention (essential for consumer social!)". Another Locket executive's post cites $14M+ ARR. — [Matt Moss LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/matt-moss-b7a95485/)
- **Celebrity Lockets (announced August 2025, after about 6 months of testing).**
  - Artists choose an audience size of 1,000–15,000 slots. In one example, Sukie Waterhouse has 5,000 fans on Locket, and 17% of them were new to the app.
  - Locket says the feature brought in "hundreds" of new users. Outlets questioned whether it would meaningfully grow the user base.
  - Sources: [TechCrunch, 2025-08-06](https://techcrunch.com/2025/08/06/photo-sharing-app-locket-is-banking-on-a-new-celebrity-focused-feature-to-fuel-its-growth); [Android Headlines, 2025-08](https://www.androidheadlines.com/2025/08/photo-sharing-app-locket-banks-on-celebrity-feature-to-drive-growth.html)
- **Audience shift.** Locket is reported to be gaining popularity with Gen Alpha as a "post-algorithm" social network. — [As Seen On substack](https://asseenonbyochuko.substack.com/p/gen-alphas-post-algorithm-social); [Bitget news](https://www.bitget.com/news/detail/12560605046319)

### Inferences
- The widget separates seeing content from opening the app. Each time a user unlocks their phone, they may see a friend's photo, so the "reason to look" happens many times a day at no cost to the user. For Rotash, a home-screen or lock-screen widget showing "today's photo from X" or "this week's collage so far (3/7)" would give the six non-shooters a daily touchpoint without requiring a session.
- Locket's two-click photo reply gives every member a cheap action. That is exactly what Rotash's non-shooters lack.
- Locket's ARR comes from a subscription (Locket Gold, per general knowledge; not verified in this session). That implies a small-circle app can monetize without an algorithmic feed.

### Gaps
- No independent (Sensor Tower or Appfigures) DAU or retention curves for Locket were found. All scale and retention figures are company claims.
- No data was found on how many widget impressions or "glances" occur per user per day.

## 3. Setlog (Japan) and similar youth apps (NauNau, Bondee, TikTok Now)

### Takeaway
Setlog is a group-based, invite-only video log. Push notifications prompt short (about 2-second) clips that cannot be edited, and these are auto-stitched into a daily vlog of the group's day. In a 2026 student survey, 91.4% of 479 students knew it and 73.7% had used it, with about 90% of users having started within the past three months. That makes it a 2026 breakout in Japan. Unlike Rotash, it gives every member several capture moments per day plus a group payoff.

### Cited Findings
- **Mechanic.** A notification arrives (reported as hourly) and the user records about a 2-second clip at that moment. Retakes and camera-roll uploads are not allowed, and there is no editing or filtering. At the end of the day the clips are automatically joined in time order into one vlog.
  - [docomo ichioshi](https://ichioshi.smt.docomo.ne.jp/articles/limited/39443); [qbei.isotop.jp ("1時間ごとに2秒の動画")](https://qbei.isotop.jp/archives/9503); [CyberLink blog](https://jp.cyberlink.com/blog/mobile/5426/setlog-app); [Coelia](https://coelia.jp/column/setlog-toha)
- **Group structure.** Users join a "log" (group) with a unique invite code.
  - Group size is reported as 2–12 people by one source and up to 20 by another (citing the App Store description). — [Coelia](https://coelia.jp/column/setlog-toha); [appbu](https://appbu.jp/setlog)
  - One 2026 article says a July 2026 update removed the once-per-hour time restriction. This is unverified; check the current App Store listing. — [shirakawaroom, 2026-08-27](https://shirakawaroom.com/2026/08/27/post-news-19858/)
- **Developer.** New Chat Inc., with offices in New York and Seoul. It is often described as "from Korea" because it spread there first. — [Coelia](https://coelia.jp/column/setlog-toha); [appbu](https://appbu.jp/setlog)
- **App Store.** The Japanese listing is id6587576438. The page showed about 1,632 ratings at search time; that is a ratings count, not users. Setlog was reported as No. 1 among free apps for consecutive days. — [App Store JP](https://apps.apple.com/jp/app/setlog/id6587576438); [CLIP](https://yourclip.life/post/about-setlog/)
- **Survey (Parente Inc., press release 2026-08).**
  - Sample: 479 students (junior high to university), surveyed inside Parente's student-discount app. The sample is not representative.
  - Awareness was 91.4% and usage 73.7% (353 people). About 90% of users started within the past three months.
  - Sources: [PR TIMES, 2026-08](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000102.000033964.html); [CommercePick](https://www.commercepick.com/archives/99672)
- **Nikkei Trendy coverage.** "About 90% of young people know it", and the service design makes UGC (user-generated content) easy to produce. — [日経クロストレンド](https://xtrend.nikkei.com/atcl/contents/18/01485/00001/)
- **Risk coverage.** TBS "Nスタ" covered privacy risks: casual 2-second clips can expose location and other personal details. — [Yahoo!ニュース / TBS NEWS DIG](https://news.yahoo.co.jp/articles/0eb4657e27920f0c5cb179154acb861095efc07c)
- **The user prompt's description of Setlog as "rotate-to-camera" was not confirmed** by any source in this session.
- **NauNau.** A location-sharing app for friends, released October 2022 as the Zenly replacement after Zenly shut down. It is reported to have passed 2.7M users in about three months. — [find-model.jp insta-lab](https://find-model.jp/insta-lab/sns-ranking-next-generation/); [原田英語](https://www.haradaeigo.com/slang/ja/NauNau)
- **Bondee.** An avatar "metaverse" SNS that spread among young Japanese users, with design cited as a reason. — [sorena.media](https://sorena.media/article/1751/). There was no reliable confirmation of a Japan shutdown.
- **TikTok Now.** A standalone TikTok app from September 2022 with dual-camera photo sharing that cloned BeReal. — [beyondmag](https://www.beyondmag.jp/posts/603). It is generally known to have been folded back into TikTok and shut down in 2023; that was not confirmed in this session.

### Inferences
- Setlog solves the "one reason per day" problem by giving everyone many small, low-effort captures (2 seconds, no editing), with an auto-generated group artifact at the end of the day. Its fast reward (same day) contrasts with Rotash's weekly payoff and one shooter per day.
- The testers' suggestion that "each person shoots 7 photos and everyone views" moves Rotash toward the Setlog and BeReal model, where every member contributes. A middle ground is possible: keep the daily "main shooter", but let the other members add an optional small contribution (a reaction, a reverse-camera photo, or a 1-photo "B-side") that appears on the collage.
- Setlog's growth (about 90% of users new within three months) is still too recent to show retention. BeReal and Lapse show that such spikes can fade within 3–6 months.

### Gaps
- No official Setlog user or DAU numbers. No retention data.
- Setlog's exact current rules (notification frequency, group cap) conflict across sources.
- No up-to-date usage data for NauNau or Bondee in 2025–2026.
- Lemon8 was not researched in this session.

## 4. Dispo, Poparazzi, Gas, Clubhouse (and Lapse): mechanics, peaks, declines

### Takeaway
All five reached No. 1 or near it through novelty and launch hype, then declined within months. Recurring causes:
- a mechanic that is fun once but has no growing reason to return (Gas, Poparazzi, Lapse)
- real-time presence demands (Clubhouse)
- incumbents copying the feature (Clubhouse, Poparazzi)
- scandal (Dispo)

TechCrunch's verdict on Lapse sums it up: growth hacking does not work "unless the product can retain its new users".

### Cited Findings
- **Dispo: mechanic.** A disposable-camera app where photos "develop" at 9am the next day. The developer called this its "founding principle". — [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/dispo-retro-disposable-camera/id1491684197)
- **Dispo: history.**
  - First released December 2019 as "David's Disposable". Relaunched in March 2021 with social features, dropping the invite-only requirement. — [Wikipedia: Dispo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dispo)
  - In March 2021 co-founder David Dobrik left after allegations against members of his Vlog Squad. Spark Capital, which had led a $20M round about a month earlier at a reported $200M valuation, cut ties. — [CNN, 2021-03-22](https://www.cnn.com/2021/03/22/tech/david-dobrik-dispo-stepping-down/index.html); [Axios, 2021-03-22](https://www.axios.com/2021/03/22/youtube-star-david-dobrik-leaving-photo-app-dispo)
  - TechCrunch in June 2021 described a "whiplash" of ups and downs, mostly caused by its namesake. — [TechCrunch, 2021-06-08](https://techcrunch.com/2021/06/08/amid-controversy-dispo-confirms-series-a-funding-high-profile-advisors-and-investors/)
- **Poparazzi: mechanic.** No selfies. Only friends (approved followers) can post photos of you to your profile; you become "your friend's poparazzi". — [Business Insider NL, 2021-05](https://businessinsider.nl/why-vcs-are-obsessed-with-no-selfie-photo-app-poparazzi-2021-5/); [Constine substack](https://constine.substack.com/p/poparazzi-photo-app-blows-up-by-banning)
- **Poparazzi: peak.** Launched May 2021 and went straight to No. 1 on the US App Store, reportedly top in 22–24 countries within a day. It was pre-hyped through TikTok, App Store preorders and TestFlight, with a reported 500k pre-installs on launch day. — [OMR](https://omr.com/de/daily/poparazzi-launch-kampagne/); [Business Insider NL](https://businessinsider.nl/why-vcs-are-obsessed-with-no-selfie-photo-app-poparazzi-2021-5/)
- **Poparazzi: decline.**
  - Usage slowed after launch. Users raised privacy concerns about auto-following every contact. — [LA Business Journal](https://labusinessjournal.com/technology/social-app-poparazzi-slowdown-after-fast-start/)
  - An analyst warned the features were easy for big platforms to copy. — [Variety VIP](https://variety.com/vip/poparazzi-is-the-hot-new-app-heres-how-it-could-cool-down-1234984405/)
  - Shutdown was announced in April/May 2023, with content downloadable until 2023-06-30, and capital returned to investors. — [TechCrunch, 2023-05-01](https://techcrunch.com/2023/05/01/once-hot-photo-sharing-social-app-poparazzi-is-shutting-down/embed/); [Poparazzi Medium "Peace Out"](https://medium.com/poparazzi/peace-out-poparazzi-34bd37e0e519)
- **Gas: mechanic and rise.** Anonymous compliment polls for high-schoolers, launched August 2022 by Nikita Bier. It reached 1M DAU within two months, with 3.1M downloads in November 2022. — [TechCrunch, 2023-10-19](https://techcrunch.com/2023/10/19/discord-kills-gas-anonymous-compliments-app-bought-nine-months-ago)
- **Gas: acquisition.** Discord acquired Gas in January 2023, mainly for the engineering team. — [Nikita Bier on X, 2023-01](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1615444742296436737); [The Information](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/discord-shutters-gas-making-it-two-in-a-row-for-gas-founder)
- **Gas: collapse.** Monthly downloads fell to 2,900 by September 2023. The shutdown was announced in October 2023 and took effect 2023-11-07. — [TechCrunch, 2023-10-19](https://techcrunch.com/2023/10/19/discord-kills-gas-anonymous-compliments-app-bought-nine-months-ago)
- **Gas: precedent.** Bier's earlier app, tbh, was sold to Facebook for about $30M in 2017 and shut down for low usage. — [Wikipedia: Tbh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tbh)
- **Clubhouse: download drop.** Sensor Tower data show monthly downloads fell about 72% from February to March 2021 (about 9.6M to 2.7M). — [Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/clubhouse-monthly-downloads-plunged-72-130048043.html). Another estimate: 9.2M in February to about 900k in June 2021, while Sensor Tower said retention among existing users stayed strong. — [Digital Music News, 2021-06-22](https://www.digitalmusicnews.com/2021/06/22/clubhouse-app-over/)
- **Clubhouse: reasons given.**
  - Invites ran out, and there was no Android app at first. — [Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/clubhouse-monthly-downloads-plunged-72-130048043.html)
  - Twitter Spaces and Facebook Live Audio Rooms copied the format. — [Android Police](https://www.androidpolice.com/why-did-clubhouse-and-other-social-audio-apps-fail/)
  - Lockdowns ended, and live audio needs people present at the same time. — [Startupik](https://startupik.com/why-clubhouse-declined-how-a-viral-app-lost-its-momentum/)
- **Clubhouse: 2023 reset.** In April 2023 it cut about half its staff and pivoted to asynchronous voice messaging among small friend groups. Its last valuation was $4B (2021). — [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/clubhouses-billion-dollar-balloon-deflates-201848479.html)
- **Lapse: mechanic.** A disposable-camera app where photos "develop" at a random later time. It was rebuilt around albums and monthly profile "photo dumps" after power users treated it as a journal. — [TechCrunch, 2023-09-26](https://techcrunch.com/2023/09/26/photo-sharing-app-lapse-hits-top-of-the-app-store-by-forcing-you-to-invite-your-friends/); [TIME](https://time.com/6334440/lapse-photo-app-instagram/)
- **Lapse: growth.** Onboarding forced invites (reportedly: add 8 friends who are already users and invite 5). Daily downloads went from about 8k to about 210k during September 2023 (Appfigures). — [TechCrunch, 2023-09-26](https://techcrunch.com/2023/09/26/photo-sharing-app-lapse-hits-top-of-the-app-store-by-forcing-you-to-invite-your-friends/); [PetaPixel](https://petapixel.com/2023/09/29/disposable-camera-app-lapse-tops-charts-by-making-users-invite-friends/)
- **Lapse: decline.** By late November 2023, downloads were down as much as 70% from the October peak. — [TechCrunch, 2023-11-27](https://techcrunch.com/2023/11/27/lapse-a-photo-app-that-forced-users-to-invite-friends-is-running-out-of-steam/)

### Inferences
- Clubhouse's 2023 pivot (to async, small groups) and Locket's durable 9M+ DAU both support Rotash's basic choice of small-group and asynchronous design. Clubhouse's live-presence requirement is a failure mode Rotash does not have.
- Gas and Poparazzi show that a mechanic built around receiving something from friends (compliments, photos of you) spikes and then dies when people stop giving. Rotash similarly depends on today's shooter actually shooting. A missed day breaks the chain for all seven members, so a fallback (reminders, a handoff to another member, or "skip and still deliver a 6-photo collage") matters.
- Lapse's move toward albums and monthly dumps suggests that a collected, periodic artifact is what users value over time. That supports Rotash's weekly collage as the core reward.

### Gaps
- No DAU or retention numbers were found for Dispo or Poparazzi; only ranking and launch claims.
- Clubhouse post-2022 DAU is not available from a primary source.
- Words With Friends turn-based retention data was not found in this session.

## 5. Patterns: delayed vs immediate reward, collaborative albums and photo dumps, streaks

### Takeaway
- **Delayed reward.** Delayed "develop" mechanics (Dispo 9am, Lapse random) create anticipation and a fixed daily reason to open. None of them retained well on that alone.
- **Collected artifacts.** Users gravitate to curated outputs (photo dumps, albums, monthly recaps).
- **Streaks.** Streaks are the strongest documented daily-open driver. Duolingo reports about 43M of its 52.7M DAUs on 7+ day streaks, and a streak-revival event helped DAU growth in 2026. Research links Snapchat streaks to FOMO, digital stress and problematic phone use in adolescents.

### Cited Findings
- **Dispo.** Photos develop at 9am the next day, "making the process of seeing your photos magical". — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/dispo-retro-disposable-camera/id1491684197)
- **Lapse.** Photos develop at a random later time. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2023/09/26/photo-sharing-app-lapse-hits-top-of-the-app-store-by-forcing-you-to-invite-your-friends/)
- **Photo dumps.** A carousel of loosely connected, casual snapshots, often after a trip or weekend. It is meant to look effortless but is "actually super curated". — [InsideHook](https://www.insidehook.com/article/internet/photo-dumps-taking-over-instagram). A dump conveys "a mood, or feeling, more than a play-by-play". — [Bustle](https://www.bustle.com/life/photo-dump-aesthetic-ideas-instagram-gen-z)
- **Carousel reach.** Hootsuite is cited as finding that carousels get 1.4x more reach and 3.1x more engagement than single posts. This is a secondary citation. — [Substack weekly roundup](https://sibiramesh.substack.com/p/the-weekly-roundup-week-of-99)
- **Duolingo streak scale.**
  - At 2025-12-31, about 43M DAUs had a 7+ day streak and about 15M had a 365+ day streak. Q4 2025 DAU was 52.7M (+30% YoY). Duolingo 10-K / [Q4 FY2025 shareholder letter](https://investors.duolingo.com/static-files/961ce633-3cee-49d0-bd7a-2c63731d45fb)
  - About 82% of DAUs on 7+ day streaks: this is my calculation from those figures, not a company statistic.
- **Duolingo retention metric.** Current User Retention Rate (the share of recently active DAUs who return the next day) hit an all-time high of 84% in Q2 2026. — [Duolingo Q2 2026 shareholder letter](https://investors.duolingo.com/static-files/3c8277ee-bc94-4f5d-9b77-0db3e46f88b8); [SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1562088/000162828026053299/q2fy26duolingo6-30x26share.htm)
- **Duolingo streak revival (Q2 2026).** A one-time event let lapsed learners restore lost streaks, and 15.4M learners revived one. The company cited it as one of three drivers of user growth. — [Duolingo Q2 2026 shareholder letter](https://investors.duolingo.com/static-files/3c8277ee-bc94-4f5d-9b77-0db3e46f88b8)
- **Duolingo has not published a clean streak vs no-streak retention comparison.** The often-quoted "+3.3% D14 retention" experiment figure has no primary citation. — [apsteq teardown](https://apsteq.com/blog/duolingo-streak-teardown/)
- **Snapchat streaks research.**
  - van Essen & Van Ouytsel (2023, *Telematics and Informatics Reports*): among early adolescents, girls were more likely to keep longer streaks, and streak engagement correlates with FOMO and problematic smartphone use. — [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/372726551_Snapchat_Streaks_-_how_are_these_forms_of_gamified_interactions_associated_with_smartphone_dependency_and_fear_of_missing_out_among_early_adolescents); [UAntwerp repository](https://repository.uantwerpen.be/docstore/d:irua:18767)
  - The same work links streaks to adolescent digital stress from notifications and peer pressure to keep a streak alive. — [UAntwerp repository](https://repository.uantwerpen.be/docstore/d:irua:18767)
- **Snap streak restore.** Snapchat offers streak restore, reportedly limited free restores with more for Snapchat+ subscribers, and group streaks cannot be restored. Only third-party sources confirmed this, and they conflict. — [unilink](https://app.unilink.us/blog/snapchat-streak-rules); [codexters](https://codexters.io/blog/how-to-restore-snapchat-streak)

### Inferences
- **The weekly collage is a delayed reward like Dispo's "develop", but stretched over 7 days.** Dispo and Lapse suggest a delay creates a reason to open at reveal time. A week is a long gap, so intermediate partial reveals (each day's slot filling in, a countdown, a "Sunday reveal" push) keep the anticipation alive.
- **A group streak fits Rotash well**, e.g. "consecutive completed weeks". It rewards the whole group, not individuals, which avoids the individual daily pressure documented for Snapchat streaks. Duolingo's revival event suggests a forgiving restore option ("freeze" or "make-up shot") keeps streaks from becoming a reason to quit.
- **Photo-dump culture validates a curated multi-photo weekly artifact** as something users want to share outward, e.g. to Instagram Stories.

### Gaps
- No data was found on Google Photos or Apple shared-album engagement.
- No official Snap data was found on how streaks affect daily opens.
- No controlled study was found comparing delayed and immediate reveal in social photo apps.

## 6. Group size effects and once-per-day / asynchronous turn-taking designs (Wordle etc.)

### Takeaway
- **Wordle and scarcity.** Wordle shows that a deliberately scarce once-a-day activity can retain massive audiences. It grew from 90 to over 300,000 players in two months, and the NYT reported 4.8 billion plays in 2023. Scarcity worked because everyone does the same thing on the same day, and a spoiler-free shareable result feeds social conversation.
- **Group size.** Research on group size is thin for social apps. In-person work suggests about 5–7 members as optimal and satisfaction peaking near 5. Founders like Locket's design for "5–15" close people.

### Cited Findings
- **Wardle on scarcity.** The breakthrough was limiting players to one game per day, which "enforced a sense of scarcity", partly inspired by NYT's Spelling Bee. His 2013 prototype allowed endless play. He deliberately avoided Silicon Valley attention tactics: "endless play, or sending them push notifications, or asking them for sign-up information." — NYT interview as quoted by [Slashdot, 2022-01-04](https://tech.slashdot.org/story/22/01/04/1412258/wordle-is-a-love-story)
- **Expert view.** Northeastern's Yakov Bart says a single daily puzzle builds anticipation and brings players back each day. A psychologist quoted by AFP (Kowert) says the one-a-day limit creates "psychological scarcity". — [Northeastern D'Amore-McKim](https://damore-mckim.northeastern.edu/news/what-makes-wordle-so-g-r-e-a-t); [Malay Mail / AFP, 2022-01-13](https://www.malaymail.com/news/tech-gadgets/2022/01/13/what-in-the-wordle-five-letter-puzzle-craze-goes-global/2034801)
- **Wordle growth.** It went from 90 to over 300,000 players in about two months (NYT, January 2022). The emoji-grid share button was added in December 2021 after Wardle saw players sharing grids by hand. NYT bought Wordle on 2022-01-31 for a "low seven-figure" sum, and it saw 4.8 billion plays in 2023. — [Wikipedia: Wordle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wordle); [Know Your Meme](https://amp.knowyourmeme.com/memes/subcultures/wordle); [Slate interview, 2022-01](https://slate.com/culture/2022/01/wordle-game-creator-wardle-twitter-scores-strategy-stats.html)
- **Group size: online vs in-person.**
  - Online collective-intelligence tasks (text chat plus shared editing) performed best at 25–35 members. The study notes earlier, mostly in-person research found 5–7 optimal. It measured task performance, not engagement. — [ScienceDirect, Decision Support Systems](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0167923622001853)
  - Secondary summaries of conversation research: dialogue turns into "sequential monologue" as groups near 10 members, and satisfaction peaks around 5. — [Conversational Swarm Intelligence paper, arXiv 2310.13702](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.13702); [conversational-leadership.net](https://conversational-leadership.net/optimal-group-size/)
- **Caps in real products.** Locket caps friends at 20 and is designed for 5–15 close people. — [Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/90818702/how-locket-a-widget-built-by-a-guy-as-a-gift-to-his-girlfriend-became-an-apple-app-store-award-winner). Setlog groups are reported at 2–12 or up to 20. — [Coelia](https://coelia.jp/column/setlog-toha)

### Inferences
- **Why Wordle's once-a-day works and Rotash's currently may not.** In Wordle every player acts every day (a synchronized shared task), and the result is shareable and comparable. In Rotash, six of the seven members only watch on most days. To borrow Wordle's model, Rotash needs a daily action for everyone, even a tiny one: guessing where the photo was taken, reacting, voting for the photo of the week, or a caption.
- **The rotation creates a turn-taking structure like Words With Friends.** The pull of "it's your turn" is strong for the person on duty. For everyone else, "the shot is in, come see it" is the counterpart (Rotash already pushes when today's photo is taken).
- **Seven members matches the in-person "5–7" optimum.** It also means each member's turn comes only once a week, so the gap between personal actions is long. Testers' proposal of "each person shoots 7 photos" adds personal actions but loses the rotation's scarcity. A hybrid keeps the scarcity: one main shot per day plus optional micro-contributions.

### Gaps
- No empirical study was found comparing engagement in 3-person vs 7-person friend groups on social apps.
- No retention data was found for Words With Friends or other asynchronous turn-based games.
- No NYT disclosure of Wordle-specific retention was found.
